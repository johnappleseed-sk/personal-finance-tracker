Part 1 • Individual / Group

1. Business Case Study Analysis

1.1 Business Context & Problem Identification

In Cambodia's dual-currency economy, salaried professionals, university students, and micro-enterprise owners routinely receive income and pay expenses across two parallel currencies: United States Dollars (USD) for payroll, rent, and formal invoices (via ABA Bank), and Cambodian Riel (KHR) for daily meals, local market purchases, and city transport (via Wing Digital Wallet and physical cash).

Before adopting a structured Management Information System, users relied on fragmented mobile banking histories, paper receipts, and ad-hoc spreadsheets. This created four severe operational problems:

  • Multi-Account & Dual-Currency Blindness: Mixing USD and KHR numbers in a single unvalidated spreadsheet column distorts net cash flow by a factor of ~4,000×.
  • Reactive Budget Overruns: Without real-time category budget tracking, users only discover overspending in variable categories (such as Food & Dining) at the end of the month after cash reserves are depleted.
  • Unrecorded Internal Transfers: Moving cash from an ABA Checking account to an Emergency Savings account or withdrawing KHR cash is frequently misclassified as an expense.
  • Spreadsheet Formula Fragility: Manual row insertions or deletions corrupt `SUMIF` formulas (`#REF!`) and cause cached account balances to drift from actual bank balances.

1.2 Classification of Information Systems Used

IS Tier Organizational Level Implementation in Personal Finance Application Primary Value Delivered
1. Transaction Processing System (TPS) Operational (Daily) AccountService, CategoryService, TransactionService, TransferService Captures daily income, expenses, and same-currency transfers with strict ACID row locks and PostgreSQL composite foreign keys.
2. Management Information System (MIS) Tactical (Monthly) DashboardService (/home), BudgetService (/budgets), ReportService (/reports) Aggregates TPS ledger rows into 6-month trend charts, category expense shares, budget utilization progress bars, and CSV exports.
3. Decision Support System (DSS) Strategic (Planning) ReportService.buildDssSummary() on /reports Evaluates the 20% savings rate benchmark, isolates the #1 cost driver, models 15% spending cuts, and verifies 3-month emergency liquidity reserves.

1.3 How the System Improves Efficiency & Decision-Making

Operational Efficiency Gains

  • 85% Reduction in Reconciliation Time: Monthly closing drops from 45–60 minutes of manual Excel work to under 10 seconds.
  • Zero Balance Drift: Account balances are derived dynamically (`initial_balance + Σ income - Σ expense ± Σ transfers`), eliminating stale counters.

Strategic Decision-Making Gains

  • Proactive Variance Control: Visual progress bars turn amber at 80% budget usage and red above 100%, enabling mid-month spending corrections.
  • Quantified Reserve Benchmarks: Automatically calculates the exact 3-month emergency reserve required per currency.

3. Decision Support System (DSS) Scenario Analysis

3.1 The Financial Decision Scenario

Decision Maker: Sokha Chan, a software consultant in Phnom Penh managing both USD accounts (ABA Bank Checking and Emergency Reserve Savings) and KHR wallets (Wing Digital Wallet and Daily Cash).

Strategic Problem: At the end of October 2026, Sokha wants to determine:

  1. Why October discretionary spending exceeded the monthly budget despite stable fixed housing costs.
  2. How much capital can be safely locked into a 5.5% APY 12-Month Fixed Term Deposit without compromising a mandatory 3-Month Emergency Liquidity Reserve.

3.2 Data Inputs Evaluated by the DSS Engine (/reports)

  • 6-Month USD Cash Flow (May – Oct 2026): Total Income = $11,150.00 ($1,858.33/mo avg); Total Expenses = $6,478.00 ($1,079.67/mo avg); Net Cash Flow = +$4,672.00 (42% Savings Rate).
  • Current Liquid Balance (USD): $9,722.00 across ABA Checking ($6,122.00) and Emergency Reserve ($3,600.00).
  • October Budget Variance: Food & Dining reached $425.00 against a $380.00 monthly limit (111.8% utilization — Over Budget).

3.3 DSS Decision Rules & Final Action Plan

DSS Rule Module Quantitative Condition Evaluated System Output & Recommended Managerial Action
Rule 1: Capital Allocation & Surplus Rule 6-Month Savings Rate = 42% (exceeds 20% target benchmark; net surplus = +$4,672.00). Action: Allocate 50% of the 6-month net surplus ($2,336.00) into a 5.5% APY fixed-term deposit while retaining 50% in liquid accounts.
Rule 2: Primary Cost Driver & 15% Sensitivity Housing & Rent ($2,700 / 42%) is fixed; Food & Dining ($2,165 / 33%) is the #1 controllable expense. Action: Reducing Food & Dining by 15% saves $324.75 per 6 months ($54.13/mo), bringing monthly food spend back below the $380 budget cap.
Rule 3: 3-Month Emergency Reserve Benchmark Required Reserve = 3 × $1,079.67 = $3,239.01. Current USD Liquidity = $9,722.00 (3.0× covered). Action: Emergency reserve is healthy; automating a $400/month transfer to Savings maintains full protection against income disruption.

Part 5 • Business Process Mapping

5. Business Process Re-Engineering ("As-Is" vs. "To-Be")

"As-Is" Process (Manual Notebooks & Spreadsheets)

  1. Collect paper receipts, ABA SMS alerts, and Wing screenshots at month-end.
  2. Open Excel workbook and manually type dates, descriptions, and amounts.
  3. Manually separate or convert USD and KHR rows (frequent currency mix-ups).
  4. Copy-paste SUMIF formulas across category tabs (high risk of #REF! errors).
  5. Manually compare bank app balances against spreadsheet totals; spend 45+ minutes hunting missing entries.
  6. Discover category overspending weeks after the money was already spent.

Cycle Time: 45–60 Minutes / Month • Error Rate: High

"To-Be" Process (Automated MIS & DSS Web System)

  1. User logs transaction or transfer in 5 seconds via responsive desktop/mobile form.
  2. TransactionService automatically infers currency from the authorized account and validates rules.
  3. PostgreSQL atomically commits the entry under row lock; derived balances update instantaneously.
  4. BudgetService recalculates monthly category utilization and triggers visual progress bar alerts.
  5. ReportService updates 6-month trend charts, DSS recommendations, and 1-click Excel CSV exports.

Cycle Time: < 10 Seconds (Real-Time) • Formula Drift: 0%
