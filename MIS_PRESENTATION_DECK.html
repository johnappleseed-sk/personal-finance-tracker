Overview

INTRODUCTION

The Personal Finance Tracker is a web-based Management Information System (MIS) and Decision Support System (DSS) built with Spring Boot & PostgreSQL to manage dual-currency finances (USD & KHR).

Core System Capabilities:

  • TPS Layer: Record income, expenses & atomic transfers across Checking, Savings, Cash, and Credit Cards
  • MIS Layer: 6-month trend reports, monthly budget vs. actual variance & category breakdown
  • DSS Layer: Automated financial health signals & interactive What-If savings simulator
  • Bilingual UI: Khmer (🇰🇭 ខ្មែរ) & English (🇺🇸 EN) + Excel CSV exports
Requirement 1 — Business Case Study Analysis (Part A)

1. BUSINESS CASE STUDY: IDENTIFY TPS, MIS & DSS

How our Personal Finance organization operates across all three information system levels:

1. OPERATIONAL LEVEL

Transaction Processing System (TPS)

• Modules: TransactionService, TransferService, AccountService
• Role: Captures daily income, expenses, and same-currency account transfers in real time.
• Guarantee: Uses Spring @Transactional and PostgreSQL ACID constraints so balances never drift.

2. TACTICAL / MANAGEMENT LEVEL

Management Information System (MIS)

• Modules: HomeController, ReportService, BudgetService
• Role: Transforms raw TPS rows into 6-month cash-flow tables, savings rate trajectories, category cost shares (%), and monthly budget utilization bars.

3. STRATEGIC / DECISION LEVEL

Decision Support System (DSS)

• Modules: ReportService.buildDssSummary() & What-If Simulator
• Role: Evaluates savings rate (≥20%), emergency runway (months), and budget overruns to prescribe concrete actions + interactive slider simulations.

Requirement 1 — Business Case Study Analysis (Part B)

1. ANALYZE PROBLEMS & RECOMMEND MIS IMPROVEMENTS

Problems Analyzed (Before MIS)

1. Slow Manual Processes & Delayed Reporting

Users spent 2–3 hours at month-end typing receipts into Excel, discovering budget overruns only after money was already spent.

2. Poor Data Flow Across Currencies (USD & KHR)

Mixing US Dollar ($) and Cambodian Riel (៛) figures in one spreadsheet column corrupted formulas and net-worth totals.

3. Unlinked Account Transfers & Error Risk

Moving funds between Checking and Savings required two manual edits, causing 15–20% unrecorded entries and balance drift.

Recommended MIS Improvements (Implemented)

1. Automated ACID Ledger & Same-Currency Transfers

Single-form transfer service debits source and credits target atomically in PostgreSQL with 100% balance accuracy.

2. Real-Time Multi-Currency MIS Aggregation (<50 ms)

Instant 6-month trend charts and KPI cards partitioned cleanly by currency (USD, KHR, EUR, GBP).

3. Proactive Budget Alerts & Interactive What-If DSS

Automated Warning (≥80%) and Over-Budget (≥100%) banners plus live slider simulation for cost cuts and 5.5% APY investment.

Requirement 2 — System Design Project (Deliverable 2: Database Design)

2B. SYSTEM DESIGN PROJECT: DATABASE DESIGN (6 TABLES)

Normalized PostgreSQL Relational Schema with Primary Keys (PK), Foreign Keys (FK), and Domain Constraints:

1. users (Owner Entity)

• PK: id (BIGSERIAL)
• email (VARCHAR 254, UNIQUE)
• password_hash (VARCHAR 100)
• display_name (VARCHAR 100)
• Rel: 1 : N with all 5 tables below

2. accounts (Financial Wallets)

• PK: id | FK: user_id → users.id
• name (VARCHAR 80)
• type (CHECKING, SAVINGS, CASH, CREDIT)
• currency (USD, KHR, EUR, GBP)
• current_balance (NUMERIC 19,2)

3. categories (Classifications)

• PK: id | FK: user_id → users.id
• name (VARCHAR 60)
• type (INCOME | EXPENSE)
• archived (BOOLEAN)
• Rel: Linked to Transactions & Budgets

4. transactions (TPS Ledger)

• PK: id | FKs: user_id, account_id, category_id
• type (INCOME | EXPENSE)
• amount (NUMERIC 19,2 CHECK > 0)
• transaction_date (DATE)
• description (VARCHAR 200)

5. budgets (Monthly Targets)

• PK: id | FKs: user_id, category_id
• budget_month (CHAR 7, YYYY-MM)
• currency (VARCHAR 3)
• limit_amount (NUMERIC 19,2 CHECK > 0)
• Unique: (user_id, category_id, month, currency)

6. transfers (Account-to-Account)

• PK: id | FKs: source_account_id, target_account_id
• currency (VARCHAR 3)
• amount (NUMERIC 19,2 CHECK > 0)
• transfer_date (DATE)
• Check: source_account_id ≠ target_account_id

Requirement 3 — Decision Support Scenario (Students Act as Managers)

3. DECISION SUPPORT SCENARIO: MAKING DECISIONS FROM DATA

Using 6-month historical data ($21,150.00 USD Income, $6,753.75 USD Expense, 68.07% Savings Rate), managers make 3 concrete decisions:

DECISION 1: COST CONTROL

Which Categories Must Be Capped?

• MIS Data Provided: In Oct 2026, Shopping reached 95.0% ($285 / $300 limit) and Food & Dining reached 82.2% ($370 / $450 limit).

• Managerial Decision: Freeze non-essential Shopping for the final 7 days of the month and cap dining out, saving +$220.00/month.

DECISION 2: CAPITAL INVESTMENT

How Much Surplus to Invest?

• MIS Data Provided: 6-month net USD cash flow is +$14,396.25 (averaging +$2,399.38/month surplus) with a 19.1-month emergency runway.

• Managerial Decision: Sweep $1,500.00/month from Checking into a 5.5% APY High-Yield Term Deposit, generating +$990.00/year in passive income.

DECISION 3: FX LIQUIDITY

How to Balance USD vs. KHR Reserves?

• MIS Data Provided: Local KHR spending averages 1,065,000 KHR/month (Food, Utilities, Transport), while KHR income averages 5,116,667 KHR/month.

• Managerial Decision: Maintain a 4,500,000 KHR working-capital buffer in ABA KHR so local bills never require unfavorable USD→KHR exchange conversions.
