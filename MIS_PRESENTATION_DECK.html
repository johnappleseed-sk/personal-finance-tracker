BUSINESS PROBLEMS

Why we developed the Personal Finance Tracker & MIS/DSS Platform (Requirement 1)

01
Disconnected Wallets & Currencies
Separate USD ($) and KHR (៛) cash and bank records make net worth hard to track.
02
Manual Spreadsheet Reporting
Typing paper receipts into Excel takes hours and delays monthly financial decisions.
03
Difficult Budget & Goal Tracking
Category spending limits and monthly savings targets lack real-time alerts.
04
Unlinked Account Transfers
Manual double-entry between Checking and Savings causes inconsistent balances.
05
No Decision Support or Forecasting
Static tables cannot simulate category cost cuts or investment yield scenarios.
