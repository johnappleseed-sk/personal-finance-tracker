Part 1 • Business Case Study Analysis

Dual-Currency Financial Visibility in Cambodia

Professionals, students, and micro-merchants in Phnom Penh receive income in USD (ABA Bank payroll) and KHR (Wing / KHQR sales) while paying daily expenses in both currencies.

Business Problems Identified Before MIS

  • Fragmented Records: Transactions scattered across bank SMS logs, paper receipts, and ad-hoc spreadsheets.
  • Currency Mixing Errors: Combining USD and KHR in one spreadsheet column distorts net worth by 4,000×.
  • Reactive Overspending: Budget overruns in Food & Dining are discovered only after cash reserves run out.
  • Spreadsheet Formula Fragility: Manual edits silently break balance formulas (`#REF!`).

How the System Improves Efficiency & Decisions After MIS

  • 85% Faster Reconciliation: Monthly closing drops from 45–60 minutes of manual Excel work to < 10 seconds.
  • 100% Balance Accuracy: Derived balances are computed directly from immutable opening balances + ledger entries.
  • Proactive Budget Alerts: Color-coded progress bars warn at 80% utilization and flag over-budget categories.
  • Data-Driven Capital Allocation: Automated DSS rules quantify 15% cost-reduction savings and 3-month liquidity targets.
Speaker Note: In Part 1, we analyzed why manual notebooks and spreadsheets fail in Cambodia's dual-currency economy. Our web-based MIS eliminates currency mixing and reduces monthly financial review time by over 85%.

Part 2B • Database Entity-Relationship Design (PostgreSQL 18)

Normalized 6-Table Relational Schema

Enforces multi-tenant isolation (`user_id`), composite foreign keys for currency safety, and zero denormalized balance drift.

1. USERS PK: id

  • email VARCHAR(254) UNIQUE
  • name VARCHAR(100)
  • password_hash VARCHAR(255)
  • Owns all downstream entities (`1:N`)

2. ACCOUNTS PK: id

  • user_id FK → users(id)
  • account_type (CHECKING, SAVINGS, CASH, CREDIT_CARD)
  • initial_balance NUMERIC(19,2)
  • currency (USD, KHR, EUR, GBP)

3. CATEGORIES PK: id

  • user_id FK → users(id)
  • name VARCHAR(100)
  • category_type (INCOME, EXPENSE)
  • Case-insensitive unique per user

4. TRANSACTIONS Ledger

  • user_id, account_id, category_id
  • transaction_type (INCOME / EXPENSE)
  • amount NUMERIC(19,2) > 0
  • currency & transaction_date

5. BUDGETS Planning

  • user_id, category_id FK
  • currency VARCHAR(3)
  • budget_month VARCHAR(7) (`YYYY-MM`)
  • limit_amount NUMERIC(19,2)

6. TRANSFERS Internal

  • source_account_id ≠ target_account_id
  • Composite FK enforces identical user_id and currency on both accounts
  • amount NUMERIC(19,2)
Speaker Note: Our ERD uses 6 normalized tables managed by Flyway migrations V1 through V5. Notice how TRANSFERS uses a composite foreign key on (user_id, account_id, currency) so cross-currency corruption is impossible at the database level.

Part 5 • Business Process Mapping ("As-Is" vs. "To-Be")

Re-Engineering Personal Financial Management

Replacing error-prone month-end manual reconciliation with real-time transactional capture and automated MIS/DSS reporting.

"As-Is" Process (Manual Notebooks & Excel) Inefficient

  • Step 1: Collect paper receipts, ABA SMS alerts, and Wing screenshots at month-end.
  • Step 2: Manually type amounts into separate Excel tabs (high typo risk).
  • Step 3: Manually convert or mix USD and KHR columns.
  • Step 4: Copy-paste `SUMIF` formulas for category totals (prone to `#REF!` errors).
  • Step 5: Discover budget overruns 2–3 weeks after overspending occurred.
⏱ Cycle Time: 45–60 min/month • High Error Rate

"To-Be" Process (Automated MIS & DSS Web App) Optimized

  • Step 1: Log transaction or transfer in 5 seconds via responsive desktop/mobile form.
  • Step 2: Service layer automatically infers currency from the selected account and validates rules.
  • Step 3: PostgreSQL atomically commits entry; derived account balances update immediately.
  • Step 4: Budget progress bars & `/reports` 6-month trend charts refresh in real time.
  • Step 5: DSS engine surfaces immediate cost-driver & reserve recommendations + 1-click CSV export.
⚡ Cycle Time: < 10 seconds (Instant) • 0% Formula Drift
Speaker Note: Part 5 compares the "As-Is" manual workflow against our "To-Be" MIS workflow. By inferring currency from the account and computing balances on the fly, we eliminate manual reconciliation bottlenecks entirely.
